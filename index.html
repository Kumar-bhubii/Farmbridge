01 / CONTEXT

The market-access gap

Small farmers often rely on fragmented local networks to find buyers and coordinate sales.

🔎

Buyer discovery

A farmer may know what is ready to harvest but not which nearby buyer needs that crop, quantity or delivery window.

📦

Fragmented quantities

A buyer seeking a larger lot may need to contact several small producers separately.

💰

Price information

Prices vary by place, grade and date. Farmers need clear, dated reference information, not unsupported promises.

🚜

Pickup coordination

Harvest timing, location, transport and buyer schedules must align for a transaction to work.

THE DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

Make local supply visible, make buyer demand discoverable, and make the next step easier to coordinate.

02 / PROPOSED SOLUTION

One bridge between supply & demand
